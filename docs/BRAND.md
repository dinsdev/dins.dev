## Personality

Crafted and warm. Calm, editorial and precise, with one playful detail. Serious about the craft, friendly in the details. Credible first for admissions, recruiters and clients; charming second for players.

Tagline: **Look closer.** It ties to Ottie's glasses and to attention to detail, and it works for the portfolio, game trailers and client pitches. "Data in. Answers out." is the portfolio's pipeline section line, not the brand tagline.

## Logo system

Files: `brand/logo/` (overview in `brand/logo/logo-sheet.html`), generated by `brand/tools/wordmark.py final` from real Fraunces outlines. Wordmark spec (Din, 2026-10-19): Fraunces weight 600, SOFT 50, WONK 0, optical size 144 for display with -15/1000 em tracking, and a small master at optical size 36 with +5/1000 em tracking for nav sizes. Variants: light, dark, mono (one color).

Concept reference: `brand/concepts/logo-directions.png` (an AI draft; the final marks are redrawn as SVG).

| Mark | Description | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark (direction 04) | "dins." in the chosen display font, lowercase, tuned spacing, the period a solid teal circle (echoes ".dev") | The text logo: site header, documents, email, anything formal |
| Lens (direction 01) | Ottie's glasses as pure geometry: two circles of even stroke, a short curved bridge, two temple stubs, one teal glint in one lens | The symbol: favicon, app-icon corner mark, the mark for any AI feature, anything tiny |
| Ottie seal (direction 03) | Ottie's face reduced to flat shapes inside a thin circular ring | The mascot badge: social avatars, stickers, game page footers |

Rules:
- The wordmark is always an SVG drawn from the chosen display font, never live text.
- Lockup: lens beside the wordmark when both appear.
- The d-lens monogram (direction 02) is dropped.
- Clear space (Claude default): at least the wordmark's x-height on every side.
- Minimum sizes (Claude defaults): wordmark 56 px wide on screen or 15 mm in print (use the small master below 120 px); lens 16 px, on its solid tile below 32 px; seal 48 px, the lens below that.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolor, add effects to, or retype the marks.

The visual companion to this guide is `brand/brand-guide.html` (built by `brand/tools/guide.py`), published privately at https://claude.ai/artifact/R8qLpurwsRu6PHmZzeDy2t.

## Color

| Role | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Background (paper) | `#F4F1EA` warm paper | `#1F1E1C` warm charcoal |
| Surface (cards, prompt bar) | `#FFFFFF` | `#2A2926` |
| Ink (main text, logo) | `#12181C` | `#ECE9E2` |
| Muted text | `#4A555C` | `#A29E95` |
| Lines (flow field, rules) | `#2B3A44` | `#7D8F8B` |
| Accent (the one brand color) | `#0F6B6B` deep teal | `#5CBFB8` calm teal |
| Warm detail | `#E0A04A` amber | `#E0A04A` amber |

Contrast on the background (WCAG): light ink 15.9, muted 6.8, accent 5.6, amber 2.0 (fails for text). Dark ink 13.7, muted 6.2, accent 7.6, amber 7.4. On the dark surface every text color stays above 5.4.

Rules:
1. Teal is the brand: the wordmark's period, the lens glint, links, buttons. Nothing competes with it.
2. Amber is decoration only and never text on the light background.
3. Ottie's browns and creams belong to Ottie and never become UI colors.
4. Each game subdomain may add one accent of its own. Paper, ink and the dins logo stay fixed so every game still reads as "by dins".

Dark mode is calm and terminal-like (in the spirit of Claude Code's dark theme): warm neutral charcoal, no glow, no blue tint. The accent stays dins teal, never Claude orange.

## Typography

Locked by Din on 2026-10-19 (option B in `brand/specimen/type-options.html`, chosen over Newsreader, Instrument Serif and Bricolage Grotesque systems).

| Role | Font | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Display serif | Fraunces (variable: weight, optical size, SOFT, WONK) | Wordmark, headlines, pull quotes |
| Sans | Instrument Sans | Body, UI, buttons, navigation |
| Mono | JetBrains Mono | Labels, stats, code, captions |

Why: Fraunces' soft serifs are the one display face that feels like it belongs in Ottie's world, while staying grown-up in ink and teal. Use its SOFT axis for warmth and keep it to display sizes so it never turns cute.

All three are OFL (free, including commercial use) and self-hosted.

Rules:
1. These three only, wherever dins appears.
2. A game's title lettering may be its own, but the dins chrome around it (nav, footer, support pages) uses the trio.
3. The wordmark is an SVG, never live text.

### Type scale

Locked by Din on 2026-10-19. Specimen: `brand/specimen/type-scale.html`. Sizes scale smoothly from a 375 px phone to a 1280 px desktop (CSS `clamp`), with no jumps at breakpoints.

| Step | Phone to desktop | Font | Line height | Tracking | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | 48 to 76 px | Fraunces 500 | 1.0 | -0.025em | The one hero line |
| H1 | 38 to 52 px | Fraunces 500 | 1.05 | -0.02em | Page titles |
| H2 | 29 to 38 px | Fraunces 500 | 1.1 | -0.015em | Section titles |
| H3 | 22 to 27 px | Fraunces 500 | 1.2 | -0.01em | Subsections |
| H4 | 18 to 20 px | Instrument Sans 600 | 1.3 | 0 | Small UI headings, card titles |
| Lead | 18 to 20 px | Instrument Sans 400, muted | 1.5 | 0 | The intro sentence under a title |
| Body | 16 to 17 px | Instrument Sans 400 | 1.65 | 0 | Reading text, max 66 characters per line |
| Small | 14 to 14.5 px | Instrument Sans 400, muted | 1.5 | 0 | Dates, meta, footnotes |
| Label | 12 to 12.5 px | JetBrains Mono 500, uppercase | 1.4 | +0.08em | Eyebrows such as "CASE STUDY · 2026" |
| Data | 12.5 to 13 px | JetBrains Mono 400, tabular digits | 1.5 | 0 | Stats, numbers, inline code |

More rules:
4. Headings use weight 500, one step lighter than the 600 wordmark, so the logo is always the boldest serif on the page. Fraunces always runs with SOFT 50 and optical sizing on.
5. Only Fraunces sets headings down to H3; H4 switches to the sans on purpose.
6. Curly apostrophes and quotes in all display text and headings (I’m, not I'm).
7. Numbers in tables and stats use tabular digits so they line up.

## Design tokens

`brand/tokens/tokens.json` is the source; `brand/tools/tokens.py` generates `brand/tokens/tokens.css` (custom properties for color in light and dark, fonts, the type scale, spacing, radius, layout and motion, plus one class per type step, `.t-display` to `.t-data`). The site imports the CSS; never hand-edit it.

Defaults set by Claude with the tokens (change through tokens.json): spacing on a 4 px base (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128), radius 6 / 10 / 14 / pill, page max 1200 px, side gutter at least 16 px, motion 150 / 250 / 400 ms with one ease curve.

## Ottie

Ottie is a cheerful brown otter with big round black glasses, drawn in a clean-outline storybook style. Reference: `brand/reference/ottie-canonical-2d.png` (canonical style) and `brand/reference/ottie-ref.jpg` (original character).

Tiers by size:

| Tier | Looks like | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Full Ottie | Storybook illustration | dins.dev hero, game art, devlog thumbnails |
| Ottie seal | Flat face in a ring | Social avatars, stickers, game page footers |
| Lens | Just the glasses | Favicon, icon corner mark, AI feature mark, anything tiny |

Role: co-host and guide. Ottie greets, explains, reacts and admits not knowing. Ottie is never the headline on dins.dev (about 25 to 30 percent of the hero height), never speaks as Din, and always talks about Din.

Always:
- The round black glasses.
- The same face and proportions. Games may add costumes or props, never change the face or glasses.

Never:
- Ottie on formal documents: resume, application PDFs, statements of purpose, email signature, invoices, contracts. These get the wordmark and at most the lens.
- Sketchy or watercolor variants, unreviewed off-model AI redraws, Ottie inside other people's logos or diagrams.

Expressions: the pose sheet `brand/concepts/ottie-poses.png` (wave, thinking, celebrating, shrug, sleeping; prompt beside it) is the reference for every later appearance. Cutouts are made at site-build time for the poses the site uses.

Ottie's voice: first person, short sentences, warm, a little nerdy, honest. For example: "I'm not sure about that one. Din would know, here's how to reach Din." No exclamation spam, no pun in every line.

## Voice (Din and dins)

- **Speaker:** "I", never "we". Game store copy may be neutral ("Help Ottie..."); devlogs stay "I".
- **Plain and specific:** concrete results, real numbers only, never invented.
- **Calm and confident:** no hype, no "passionate", no exclamation points in Din's voice.
- **Dry humor, sparingly.** Ottie carries most of the playfulness.
- **Writing rules:** no em dashes or en dashes as separators, no buzzwords (elevate, seamless, unleash, journey, tapestry, "not just X but Y").

## Names and spelling

- Always lowercase **dins**, including at the start of a sentence.
- **dins.dev**: no www, no capitals.
- Handle: **dinsdev** on every platform. Optional Bluesky handle: **@dins.dev**.
- Display name on profiles: **dins**.
