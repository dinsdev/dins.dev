Logo

Three marks, each with one job. The wordmark is the logo. The lens is the symbol for small places. The Ottie seal is the playful badge. All three are vector files drawn from real font outlines and code, never typed or traced by hand.

Wordmark
"dins." in Fraunces 600, SOFT 50, display optical size. The period is a solid teal dot that echoes .dev.

Ottie's glasses as geometry: two circles, a curved bridge, temple stubs, rims at 0.14 of the lens diameter, a teal glint in the right lens.

Ottie

The mascot and co-host. A cheerful brown otter with big round black glasses who greets, explains, reacts and admits not knowing. Ottie talks about Din and never speaks as Din.

Always

  • The round black glasses.
  • The same face and proportions. Games may add costumes or props.
  • About 25 to 30 percent of the hero height on dins.dev. A co-host, never the headline.
  • New appearances start from the pose sheet.

Never

  • On formal documents: resume, applications, statements of purpose, email signature, invoices, contracts.
  • Sketchy or watercolor variants, or unreviewed off-model redraws.
  • Inside other people's logos or diagrams.
  • Speaking as Din.

Voice

Plain, specific, calm. Din writes as "I", because dins is one person. Ottie carries most of the playfulness.
